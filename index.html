Born from Brotherhood,
Forged in Iron & Snow

It started in the summer of 2014 when 5 close-knit rider friends set out with loaded Royal Enfield 350s, handwritten maps, and a simple passion: to conquer the treacherous dirt tracks of Ladakh and Nubra Valley on our own terms.

Over a decade later, Himalayan Ranger has evolved into a premier adventure expedition club. Yet, our founding principle remains untouched: small ride groups, uncompromised mechanical back-up, medical support on high altitudes, and genuine camaraderie.

Oxygen & Medic Vehicles

Every group trip is flanked by a 4x4 support truck carrying medical-grade oxygen cylinder units and trained first responders.

Master Mechanic On Board

High altitudes test engines. Our lead mechanic ensures your machine never stalls at 15,000+ feet.
